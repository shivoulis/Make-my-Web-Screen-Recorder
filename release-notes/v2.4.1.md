## Make my Web Screen Recorder 2.4.1

### Fixed
- **Browser tab recording stays on your tab.** Before, switching to another tab recorded that tab instead. Now, while your chosen tab is out of sight (another tab selected, the browser minimised, or another window in front of it), the video holds on the last picture of your tab, and **your microphone and the computer's sound keep recording**. Handy for calls: everyone's voices are kept while you check something else. The control bar shows **Tab hidden** and the frame turns amber until you go back to the tab.

### Install
Download `MakeMyWebScreenRecorder-Setup-2.4.1.exe` below and run it (no admin rights needed). Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet: click **More info → Run anyway**.

Already on 2.2.0 or later? You'll be offered this update when you open the app.
